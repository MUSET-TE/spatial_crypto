Name of Quantlet: 'NARCH_estimation'

Published in: 'Spatial Effects and Uncertainty in Cryptocurrencies: the case of Network ARCH Models'

Description: 'Stage 2 of the pipeline: the dynamic network ARCH model estimated by
              quasi-maximum likelihood under each of the three neighbourhood
              definitions, reproducing the full selection table of the paper. The model
              carries three parameters: rho for contemporaneous spatial dependence,
              gamma for temporal persistence and delta for the spatiotemporal
              transmission of neighbours past volatility. Two estimation points are
              handled explicitly. The contemporaneous spatial lag is endogenous by
              construction, since coin j log-volatility today is a regressor in coin i
              equation while coin i is a regressor in coin j, so least squares is
              inconsistent rather than merely inefficient. Identification comes from the
              log-determinant term of the quasi-likelihood: without it the criterion
              reduces to a sum of squares that the optimiser can shrink by driving rho
              towards one, so spillover would be reported for any series at all. All six
              cells of the grid are reported rather than only the two selected, because
              that is the evidence that the price versus volume contrast does not depend
              on how the neighbourhood was defined: rho stays in 0.346 to 0.500 for
              prices and in 0.116 to 0.280 for volumes, and the two ranges never meet.
              A coin left with an all-zero row of W, which happens to dexe under the
              threshold rule on volumes, is filtered without the neighbour terms, since
              those drop out of its mean equation by construction.'
