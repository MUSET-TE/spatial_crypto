Name of Quantlet: 'NARCH_data'

Published in: 'Spatial Effects and Uncertainty in Cryptocurrencies: the case of Network ARCH Models'

Description: 'Builds the balanced panel of 50 cryptocurrencies over 1517 consecutive
              trading days, from 6 January 2021 to 2 March 2025, that underlies the
              network ARCH study, and produces the two descriptive figures motivating
              it. Starting from the top 100 coins by market capitalisation, a coin is
              retained only if it is fully observed on price, volume and market
              capitalisation over a common window, and the window is chosen to maximise
              the number of fully observed coin-day observations. Six coins are excluded:
              three for insufficient history, two for discontinuous listings and one for
              long runs of zeros that become non-finite under the log transform. Gaps are
              never imputed, since interpolation would insert smooth artificial structure
              into the co-movement of volatility that the study sets out to measure. The
              script reports that the pipeline finiteness guard never fires on the
              retained panel, so no value in it has been replaced. Prices and volumes are
              converted to daily logarithmic changes, which are additive over time and
              symmetric in gains and losses. The average pairwise return correlation is
              0.46 for prices and 0.34 for volumes.'

Keywords: 'balanced panel, data cleaning, missing data, window selection, log returns,
           volatility clustering, cryptocurrency, replicability, descriptive statistics'

Author: 'Mihai-Adrian Achim, Stefana Belbe, Codruta Mare, Philipp Otto'
