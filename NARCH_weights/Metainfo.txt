Name of Quantlet: 'NARCH_weights'

Published in: 'Spatial Effects and Uncertainty in Cryptocurrencies: the case of Network ARCH Models'

Description: 'Builds and visualises the two spatial weight matrices used to define
              cryptocurrency neighbourhoods in the network ARCH model. Neighbours are
              derived from the correlation matrix of daily log returns, since coins have
              no geographical location. Two definitions are compared: an absolute
              correlation threshold at 0.15, selected for the price model, and a
              k-nearest-neighbour rule with k = 5, selected for the volume model. Both
              matrices are row-standardised, so each row sums to one and a weight is the
              share of a coin neighbourhood accounted for by one neighbour. Colour is
              scaled by quantile within each panel, because row standardisation ties the
              size of a weight to the number of neighbours, a median of 45 links for
              prices against exactly five for volumes.'

Keywords: 'spatial weight matrix, adjacency matrix, row standardisation, correlation
           network, k-nearest-neighbour, cryptocurrency, volatility network,
           spatial ARCH, heatmap'

Author: 'Mihai-Adrian Achim, Stefana Belbe, Codruta Mare, Philipp Otto'

See also: 'NARCH_estimation, NARCH_data, https://github.com/MUSET-TE/spatial_crypto'
