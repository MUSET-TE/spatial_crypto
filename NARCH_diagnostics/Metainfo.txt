Name of Quantlet: 'NARCH_diagnostics'

Published in: 'Spatial Effects and Uncertainty in Cryptocurrencies: the case of Network ARCH Models'

Description: 'Specification checks and the dynamic stability condition for the two
              selected network ARCH models. Three questions are asked. Whether any
              network dependence is left in the residuals, tested with Moran I computed
              cross-sectionally on each day. Whether the volatility clustering has been
              absorbed, tested with Ljung-Box at lag 20 and with ARCH-LM on the squared
              residuals. And whether the estimated process settles rather than explodes,
              which requires the spectral radius of the reduced-form transition matrix
              to be strictly below one. Because W is row-standardised its largest
              eigenvalue is one, so the condition reduces to gamma plus delta over one
              minus rho, and the script verifies that closed form against the actual
              largest eigenvalue of the 50 by 50 matrix rather than asserting that they
              agree. Both selected models are stable, at 0.894 for prices and 0.224 for
              volumes. The squared residuals are clean on both counts, while the
              residual levels remain serially correlated for 96 and 98 percent of coins,
              which the paper reports as a known limitation and which this Quantlet
              shows rather than hides. Moran I is computed on the cross-section of
              per-coin mean residuals, the convention behind Table 2, and reproduces its
              four figures: +0.03 and -0.05 for prices, +0.04 and -0.07 for volumes, all
              insignificant. The stricter day-by-day variant is reported alongside it.'
