Name of Quantlet: 'NARCH_meanfilter'

Published in: 'Spatial Effects and Uncertainty in Cryptocurrencies: the case of Network ARCH Models'

Description: 'Stage 1 of the two-stage network ARCH pipeline: a spatiotemporal ARMA mean
              filter estimated coin by coin by maximum likelihood, carrying the
              contemporaneous and the lagged row-standardised neighbour return as
              external regressors. The stage exists to break a circularity. The
              neighbourhood matrix is built from return correlations, so estimating
              volatility dependence directly on raw returns would recover the structure
              that was inserted by hand, and the spatial parameter would measure return
              co-movement rather than volatility transmission. Filtering the conditional
              mean first leaves innovations explained neither by a coin own past nor by
              what its neighbours did today or yesterday, and only their magnitude is
              passed to stage 2. The contemporaneous neighbour regressor is admissible
              here because cryptocurrency markets trade continuously, with no closing
              auction behind which a lagged effect could hide. The filter removes a median
              of 55 percent of return variance for prices and 43 percent for volumes,
              while leaving the dependence in the squared innovations intact, which is
              exactly what stage 2 is meant to model.'

Keywords: 'ARMA, mean filter, spatiotemporal, external regressors, innovations,
